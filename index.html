Welcome to Baxter & Greene

A Peaceful Culinary Oasis Above Henry Street

Located inside Dunnes Stores at the heart of the Ilac Shopping Centre, Baxter & Greene offers a bright, spacious, and immaculate setting away from the busy city streets.

Whether you're stopping by for our famous 5-item cooked Irish breakfast under €6, enjoying a slice of freshly baked lasagne, or sipping a signature coconut cappuccino on our outdoor rooftop terrace, we provide honest, wholesome comfort food prepared fresh every single day.
